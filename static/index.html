broken by design.

The key safety insight: LLM writes zero ledger entries.
DeepSeek is only permitted to propose a match. The deterministic safety gate independently verifies the paisa math and T+2 banking SLA before anything is committed. If the numbers don't balance, the system abstains and queues the exception — no hallucinated commits, ever.

Under the Hood

A three-stage decision pipeline.

Every transaction goes through three gates. Only if all three agree does a match get committed. This makes false positives mathematically impossible.

Stage 01
Deterministic Engine
4-way proof: Order ID + UTR + Paisa Math ± ₹0.01 + Banking SLA T+2
Order ID exact match
UTR cryptographic link
MDR math (UPI/Card/Amex)
T+2 banking calendar
Stage 02
DeepSeek LLM Forensic
Only for exceptions. Active batch decomposition, narration matching, anomaly diagnosis.
Fuzzy bank narration match
Batch delta forensic audit
Confidence scoring 0–100%
Stage 03
Safety Gate + HITL
LLM confidence ≥85% AND paisa math verified. Otherwise: abstain → exception queue.
AUTO_COMMIT (verified)
ABSTAINED (exception)
USER_APPROVED (human)
Batch Disaggregation
When one bank credit covers 10 orders, the engine separates and auto-commits the 9 clean payouts while isolating only the 1 anomalous outlier.
Multi-Instrument MDR
UPI (0%), Debit Card (0.5%), Credit Card (2%), Amex (3.5%) — contract rates are dynamically applied per transaction, not hard-coded.
Indian Banking Calendar SLA
T+2 SLA is calculated in business banking days (skipping weekends and RTGS holidays), not raw calendar days.
Immutable Audit Trail
Every decision — auto-committed, proposed, or abstained — is logged with a complete trace of the reasoning chain for compliance review.
